Webcam Motion Recorder

Uses MediaPipe Holistic + Kalidokit to drive the VRM live from your webcam. Records body pose only (arms/spine/hips) as deltas from a calibrated neutral pose -- tag an expression afterward, same as the Mixamo converter. Both libraries here are community-standard but officially deprecated with no actively maintained replacement for this exact job -- expect to need to tune the mirror toggle and calibration once you actually try it.
